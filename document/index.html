Coursework Research Topics

Detailed breakdown of the 12 technical questions analyzed in the assignment across four thematic architectural domains:

01 / Supercomputing & Sovereign AI Questions 1–3
Q01

Yotta Data Center & Supercomputing

Analysis of Yotta NM1 and the Shakti Cloud infrastructure, detailing liquid-cooled NVIDIA H100 clusters and high-density hyperscale operations.

Q02

India AI Summit & Compute Infrastructure

Exploration of national sovereign AI initiatives, compute subsidies, public-private GPU allocation models, and domestic computing ecosystems.

Q03

NPTEL Computer Architecture Curriculum

Core takeaways from national engineering curricula covering instruction set architectures (ISA), pipeline hazards, and memory hierarchies.

02 / Processing Units & History Questions 4–7
Q04

CPU vs GPU vs TPU Paradigms

Contrasting latency-optimized general compute (CPU), throughput-optimized parallel matrix engines (GPU), and systolic array tensor processors (TPU).

Q05

Intel & AMD Historical Founders

Historical evolution of the x86 duopoly: Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore (Intel) alongside Jerry Sanders (AMD) and their lasting architectural rivalry.

Q06

Turing Award & Computing Pioneers

Survey of seminal ACM A.M. Turing Award laureates who shaped compiler theory, relational databases, RISC microprocessors, and deep learning.

Q07

GPU vs CPU Architectural Tradeoffs

Comparative analysis of control logic complexity, branch prediction overhead, and SIMD/SIMT thread execution hierarchies.

03 / Systems Theory & Foundations Questions 8–10
Q08

Programming Language Selection Criteria

Balancing hardware abstraction, memory management safety, compiler optimization passes, and execution speed when selecting system languages.

Q09

The Imitation Game & Alan Turing

Theoretical implications of the Bombe electromechanical cryptanalysis machine, Enigma rotor permutation mechanics, and the Universal Turing Machine.

Q10

Stampeding Herd Problem in Systems

Investigation into concurrent cache invalidation spikes, mutex starvation, and mitigation strategies such as jittered TTLs and mutex collapsing.

04 / Modern Silicon Architectures Questions 11–12
Q11

Apple M4 CPU Core Organization

Analysis of TSMC 3nm ARMv9 architecture, 10-core SoC topology (Performance vs Efficiency cores), Next-Gen Neural Engine, and Unified Memory architecture.

Q12

Raspberry Pi Architecture & SBCs

Single-board computer engineering: Broadcom BCM2712 SoC, quad-core Cortex-A76 cores, PCIe 2.0 bus topology, and GPIO controller integration.
